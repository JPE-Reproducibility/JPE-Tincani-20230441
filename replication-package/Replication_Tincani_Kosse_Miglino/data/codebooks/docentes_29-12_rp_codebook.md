# Codebook Note for `Docentes 29-12_rp.dta`

This note documents the confidential teacher survey dataset `Docentes 29-12_rp.dta`. The dataset is stored in:

`confidential-data-not-for-publication/raw/Teachers_and_principals/Docentes 29-12_rp.dta`

## General Description

This dataset contains teacher-level survey responses from schools in the study. The survey records school identifiers, teachers' subject responsibilities, the fourth-year high school classes they teach, course modality, teacher experience with the current student cohort, time spent with students, teacher absences, and instructional practices or content coverage in mathematics and language classes.

The variables are mostly survey-question fields named with question numbers, such as `Q4_1`, `Q105_1`, and `Q129_x5`. The labels in the source file provide the original Spanish survey-question text; the descriptions below summarize blocks of variables.

## Approximate Variable Blocks

- `Q44_1_0` identifies the school using the RBD school code.
- `Q1` records general teacher information, including the subject or subjects taught at the school.
- `Q4_*` variables record which fourth-year high school courses the respondent teaches in mathematics.
- `Q6_*` variables record which fourth-year high school courses the respondent teaches in language.
- `Q51` and `Q52` describe the modality of the mathematics and language courses taught by the respondent.
- `Q53`, `Q74`, `Q76`, and `Q71` capture teacher-student contact, time-use, or attendance-related information, including how long the teacher has taught the current fourth-year students, weekly time spent with students, and teacher absences.
- `Q105_*` through `Q112_*` describe mathematics instruction and content coverage for fourth-year high school classes, including whether specific curricular topics were covered and how students engaged with those topics.
- `Q120_*` through `Q129_*` describe language instruction and content coverage for fourth-year high school classes, including reading, writing, literary genres, text analysis, and related student engagement or skill-development items.
