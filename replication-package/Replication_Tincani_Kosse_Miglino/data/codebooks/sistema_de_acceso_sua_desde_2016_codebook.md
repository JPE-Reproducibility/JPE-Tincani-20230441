# Codebook Note for `Sistema de Acceso y SUA desde 2016 100423`

This note documents the dataset `Sistema de Acceso y SUA desde 2016 100423`. The dataset identifies higher education institutions that are part of the Sistema Unico de Admision (SUA), the centralized college admissions system, from 2016 onward.

## File Location

The dataset is stored in:

`confidential-data-not-for-publication/raw/List_selective_colleges`

## Variables

- `CLASIFICACIÓN INSTITUCIÓN NIVEL 3`
- `CÓDIGO DE INSTITUCIÓN`
- `NOMBRE INSTITUCIÓN`
- `MAT_2016`
- `MAT_2017`
- `MAT_2018`
- `MAT_2019`
- `MAT_2020`
- `MAT_2021`
- `MAT_2022`
- `MAT_2023`
- `MAT_2024`

## Variable Summary

- `CLASIFICACIÓN INSTITUCIÓN NIVEL 3`: institutional classification at level 3.
- `CÓDIGO DE INSTITUCIÓN`: institution code.
- `NOMBRE INSTITUCIÓN`: institution name.
- `MAT_2016`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2016.
- `MAT_2017`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2017.
- `MAT_2018`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2018.
- `MAT_2019`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2019.
- `MAT_2020`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2020.
- `MAT_2021`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2021.
- `MAT_2022`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2022.
- `MAT_2023`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2023.
- `MAT_2024`: indicator for participation or membership in the centralized admissions system in 2024.

## Notes

The `MAT_*` variables are year-specific dummy variables coded with text values `SI` and `NO`.
