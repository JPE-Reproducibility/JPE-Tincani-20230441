## General Description

Taken together, these variables describe applicant-level records from the Chilean PACE admissions process. They include anonymized student identification, demographic information, admission-process timing, application preferences, program or degree identifiers, PACE participation or eligibility information, and admission outcomes such as selection status and position in the admissions list.

The variables also cover students' secondary-school background, including school identifiers, type of school or educational track, administrative dependency, graduation status, and year of high school completion. Academic preparation is represented through high school grades, grade percentiles, NEM scores, school ranking scores, standardized university-admission test scores across subject areas, and combined or percentile-based performance measures.

Finally, the dataset includes socioeconomic and geographic information, such as family income category and the applicant's region, province, commune, and city. Overall, the variables support analysis of how students' school background, academic performance, application behavior, socioeconomic context, and location relate to PACE eligibility, application choices, and admissions outcomes.
